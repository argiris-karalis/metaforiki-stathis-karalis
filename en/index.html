Frequently Asked Questions

How is the cost of a move calculated?

The cost depends on the volume of the items, the distance between the two locations, the number of floors (and whether there is an elevator), the need to use a lifting machine, as well as any additional services (e.g., unpacking, assembly).

Do you provide packaging materials (filler)?

Yes, we have all the necessary professional packing materials (cardboard boxes, bubble wrap, stretch film, special bags for mattresses and clothing). We can handle the packing entirely ourselves or supply you with the materials.

When should a lifting machine be used?

A hoist is recommended when there is large or heavy furniture that does not fit in the elevator or down the stairs, or when moving items to upper floors. It ensures faster transport and eliminates the risk of damage to the furniture and the apartment building’s common areas.

How far in advance should I schedule an appointment for the move?

We recommend that you contact us 5–10 days in advance to secure the date and time that works best for you, especially during peak periods (the end and beginning of the month).

Do you provide on-site cost estimates?

Absolutely! We offer a **free on-site visit** to accurately assess the volume and specific details of your move, so that we can provide you with a final, transparent quote with no hidden fees.
